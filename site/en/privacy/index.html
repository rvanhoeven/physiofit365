What we collect

Only what you enter in the contact form: name, email, phone number, town or area, the subject of your question, your message, whether you prefer an answer by phone or email, and the language of the page you used. We also store the time of your request.

Why we collect it

To answer your question and, if you wish, to arrange a home visit. We rely on your consent, which you give by ticking the box before sending the form.

Where it is stored

Requests are stored in a database hosted in the European Union. Only PhysioFit365 can read them. We do not sell or share your data for marketing.

How long we keep it

No longer than needed to deal with your request and any follow-up care.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, by emailing info@physiofit365.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the Spanish data protection authority (AEPD).
