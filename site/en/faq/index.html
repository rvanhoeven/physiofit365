Frequently asked questions

What is a physiotherapist?

Physiotherapists are university-trained experts in diagnosing and treating injuries, prescribing exercise, preventing injury and rehabilitation, across many areas of sport, health and fitness.

Are there physiotherapy specialists?

Yes. PhysioFit365 works with professionals who have advanced training in manual therapy, sport therapy, work-related therapy and other disciplines.

Do you really treat at home?

Yes. We come to you in and around Rojales. Treating you in your own surroundings is more comfortable, and a session usually lasts 45 to 60 minutes, sometimes shorter or longer.

Which languages do you speak?

Mellany speaks English, Dutch and Spanish, so you can describe your complaint in the language you are most comfortable with.

Which complaints can you treat?

Common reasons are pain, recovery after surgery, muscle strengthening, work-related injuries and sport injuries. Not sure? Send us a message and we will advise you.

How do I get in touch?

Use the contact form, call us or email info@physiofit365.com. We are available Monday to Friday, 15:00 to 21:00.

Not sure which treatment fits?

Describe your complaint and we will advise you.
