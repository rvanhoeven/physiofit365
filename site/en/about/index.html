About PhysioFit365

PhysioFit365 offers physiotherapy at home on the Costa Blanca, combining traditional physiotherapy with modern manual therapy.

Our philosophy

Therapy works best when people are treated as whole human beings, not as separate body parts. We look for the root causes of your complaint and treat those.

Mellany Ginny van Hoeven

Physiotherapist · Registered nurse

Mellany started PhysioFit365 in 2020 to create a practice that combines the best of traditional physiotherapy with the latest principles and techniques of manual therapy.

Education

Mellany graduated as a physiotherapist from UCH CEU University in Elche (Spain) in 2017. Three years later she also graduated as a fully registered nurse. She has a special interest in manual therapy and sport rehabilitation.

Professional registration

Illustrious Official College of Physiotherapists of the Valencian Community, number 6048.

Career

During her seven years at university, Mellany worked as a physiotherapist in various public and private hospitals, as well as in private practices, institutes and government rehabilitation centres.

Qualifications

  • Graduate diploma in physiotherapy (4-year university programme)
  • Graduate diploma in nursing (4-year programme, completed in 3 years)

Languages

English, Dutch and Spanish

Our vision

To be a practice that attracts, rewards and keeps highly skilled physiotherapy staff, and that keeps developing effective treatment methods.

Our mission

As primary healthcare professionals we help our clients resume their lifelong pursuit of health, fitness and well-being.

High quality

We take a goal-oriented approach to healing. Whatever your goals are, we want to help you achieve them.

Patient care

Hands-on treatment plus the education you take home with you, so you become an active participant in your recovery and future health.
